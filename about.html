about

The Computational Neuroimaging and Perception Team is directed by Dr. Eli Merriam and located in the Laboratory of Brain and Cognition.

Our lab employs converging approaches from human psychophysics, computational neuroscience,functional brain imaging to elucidate the link between visual perception and measures of neural activity.

Our work is supported by the Intramural Research Program of that National Institutes of Mental Health.
